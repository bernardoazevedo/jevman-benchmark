jevman: a Pac-Man benchmark for decision models

Six AI decision models played Pac-Man in real time against the classic arcade ghosts, 24 games each. Watch their games and see the leaderboard below, or play against them yourself.

Method

  • Each model plays Pac-Man against the classic scripted ghosts until it loses three lives or 5 minutes pass. 24 games per model.
  • At every junction the game asks the model one question, with the maze, pellets and ghosts as state. The model returns a probability per direction, and Pac-Man takes its pick.
  • An answer that takes longer than 2 seconds is replaced by a simple backup rule, counted under backup moves.
  • Scores are means with a 95% margin of error (±2 standard errors). Models within each other's margin are tied.
  • Every game is recorded and replays exactly, so any result can be checked.
